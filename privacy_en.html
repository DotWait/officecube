Privacy Policy

Effective: October 23, 2026　|　Publisher: DotWait　|　Product: OfficeCube for Windows (desktop application)

Our promise: your documents never leave your own computer.All document processing — including receipt OCR — runs locally and offline on your device. We never collect, upload, inspect, or store any of your documents, spreadsheets, PDFs, images, or their contents.

1Scope

This policy applies to the Windows desktop application “OfficeCube” published by DotWait (the “App”), including all 13 tools: Excel merge/split, large-file viewer, text cleaner, data sanitizer/audit; PDF split/merge, compression, redaction & watermark; Word-to-PDF with layout fixing, sensitive-word scanner, document formatter, mail merge; Markdown-to-PPT; and invoice/bank-receipt OCR archiving.

The App is distributed as an MSIX package through the Microsoft Store.

2Local-first processing (data stays on device)

  • File access only occurs when you explicitly choose files in the system picker or drag files in;
  • All processing (merge, split, cleaning, redaction, compression, OCR, conversion) runs in local memory and temp folders, cleaned up afterwards;
  • OCR uses an on-device AI model (ONNX Runtime) shipped with the App and never calls a cloud service;
  • Works offline; only license verification needs a connection;
  • No registry writes, no background services; uninstalling the MSIX removes local data.

3The only network use: Microsoft Store licensing

Purchases complete entirely in Microsoft checkout; the App never touches your bank card, payment account, or billing address. These requests contain no file names or document content.

4Data stored on this device

  • Preferences: UI language (Simplified Chinese / Traditional Chinese / English / Japanese), theme, default output folder;
  • Job status and recent job history (file names and status only);
  • The invoice archiver's local SQLite ledger with recognized fields;
  • Output files are saved where you choose; the App keeps no copies.

Development builds have a local Pro demo switch on this machine only; the Store release uses the Microsoft license.

5Information we do NOT collect

  • No account or login; no name, phone number, or email;
  • No third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs;
  • No advertising identifiers, no profiling, no selling or sharing data;
  • We do not read unrelated folders or scan the disk.

6Subscriptions & lifetime license

Three Pro options unlock exactly the same features:

  • Monthly: auto-renewed monthly via the Microsoft Store; cancel anytime in your Microsoft account, access returns to Free at period end;
  • Yearly: auto-renewed annually, with the same cancellation rules;
  • Lifetime: a one-time payment for a permanent unlock; restore on a new device;
  • After expiry/refund the App returns to Free limits (up to 3 files per run, serial processing); produced files and your local ledger are never affected;
  • Renewals, refunds and invoices are handled by Microsoft under Store policies.

7Third parties & components

  • Microsoft Store / Windows services for licensing, purchase and updates;
  • Document-processing components (OpenXML, PdfSharpCore, pdfium, PDFtoImage, SixLabors.ImageSharp, SkiaSharp, ClosedXML, NPOI, SQLite, RapidOCR/ONNX Runtime) run entirely locally with no online telemetry.

8Data security

Because files never leave the device, cloud-breach risk does not apply. We recommend encrypting highly sensitive outputs yourself. Passwords for encrypted PDF/Word files exist only in memory for the current job and are never persisted.

9Children's privacy

The App is a general productivity tool, is not designed for children under 14, and does not knowingly collect personal information.

10Policy changes

Updates are posted on this page with a revised effective date; material changes are announced in release notes. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
